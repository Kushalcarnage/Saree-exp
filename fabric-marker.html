Add a saree to the website

Choose a photo, mark the fabric, fill in the details, then copy the result into index.html. No coding needed.

1Choose the saree photo

Best: the saree laid flat or folded, straight from above, in daylight, showing the body fabric and the border.

2Mark the fabric for the try-on mirror

Yellow box = body fabric (avoid folds, tags and hands). Red box = the border band, from where it meets the body to its outer edge.
